## Topography (Slope) & Laterite Formation

Range: 2–20°. Mature laterite landscape typical of eastern Halmahera.
- **Optimal Laterite Formation**: Nickel laterites form optimally on gentle slopes (typically 5–15°). 
  - Slopes < 5° (flat): Poor drainage leads to waterlogging and formation of smectite clays (nontronite), which dilutes grade.
  - Slopes > 15-20° (steep): Physical erosion outpaces chemical weathering, stripping the laterite profile before it can fully form.
- **Sources**: 
  - SRTM 30m DEM analysis of Weda Bay region (USGS EarthExplorer).
  - Golightly, C.G. (1981). *Nickeliferous laterite deposits*. Economic Geology 75, 710–735.
  - Freyssinet, P. et al. (2005). *Base Metal Metallogeny: Nickel Laterites*. Economic Geology 100th Anniversary Volume.
  - Dalvi, A.D. et al. (2004). *The past and the future of nickel laterites*. PDAC International Convention.

## ML Training Data: Forward Model Approach

The labels used for ML training are generated by a stochastic forward model, not from the observed features directly. This mirrors real exploration: we observe noisy surface measurements (geochem, magnetics) and try to predict the hidden true nickel grade. The same architecture works unchanged with real field data.

The hidden true Ni grade is a function of lithology and spatial position, corrupted by Gaussian noise to simulate measurement error and natural variability. The ML model (XGBoost) must learn to filter this noise — that is genuine learning.

## Metallurgical Suitability (RKEF vs HPAL)

Processing route logic is based on industry-standard feed requirements for Indonesian nickel smelters:
- **RKEF (Rotary Kiln Electric Furnace)**: Requires saprolite ore with high Ni (typically >=1.5%) and low Fe (<30%). High Fe creates slag volume issues in the electric furnace.
- **HPAL (High-Pressure Acid Leach)**: Designed for limonite ore with low Ni (~0.8–1.5%) but high Fe (>=30%) and recoverable Cobalt for EV battery precursors.
- **Source**: Anne, M. et al. (2020). *Nickel Laterite Processing Technologies and the Rise of HPAL in Indonesia*. Journal of Sustainable Metallurgy.

## Data Sanitization & QA/QC Limits

To handle real-world field data anomalies before ML inference, the following hard caps are implemented based on theoretical geological maximums:
- **Maximum Nickel (Ni%)**: 5.0%. Even the richest supergene enrichment zones rarely exceed 3-4% Ni in bulk mining blocks. Anything >5% is treated as a laboratory error and capped.
- **Maximum Cobalt (Co%)**: 0.5%. Natural cobalt in laterites peaks around 0.1-0.2%. Values >0.5% are capped.
- **Magnetometer Bounds**: Baseline Earth magnetic field at Halmahera is ~43,000 nT. Negative readings or massive spikes (>100,000 nT) are considered equipment/sensor errors and smoothed to the baseline.
- **Source**: KCMI (Komite Cadangan Mineral Indonesia) Code 2017 Guidelines for reporting Exploration Results.

## K3 / Exploration Safety Standards

Field access and medevac risk logic is based on standard Indonesian mining safety guidelines:
- **Slope > 25°**: Triggers high landslide risk warnings and requires specialized access (winch/heli).
- **Distance to Road > 2000m**: Triggers poor medevac access warnings.
- **Source**: Kepmen ESDM 1827 K/30/MEM/2018 (Guidelines for the Implementation of Good Mining Engineering Principles) regarding exploration safety and emergency response preparedness.

## Generative ESG & Permitting Framework

The Auto-ESG Permit drafting logic strictly follows these Indonesian legal frameworks to guarantee zero-hallucination outputs:
- **Forestry Usage (PPKH)**: *Permen LHK No. 7 Tahun 2021* (Perencanaan Kehutanan, Perubahan Peruntukan Kawasan Hutan dan Perubahan Fungsi Kawasan Hutan, serta Penggunaan Kawasan Hutan). Used to draft the 1:1 Rehabilitasi DAS obligations for grids inside Hutan Lindung/Produksi.
- **Environmental Viability (AMDAL vs UKL-UPL)**: *Permen LHK No. 4 Tahun 2021* (Daftar Usaha dan/atau Kegiatan yang Wajib Memiliki Analisis Mengenai Dampak Lingkungan Hidup, Upaya Pengelolaan Lingkungan Hidup dan Upaya Pemantauan Lingkungan Hidup). Used to trigger AMDAL recommendations if grids intersect 500m river buffers.
